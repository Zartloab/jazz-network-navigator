# Hamed-First Product Vision

## First user

The first real user is Hamed Sadeghi, an independent artist operating several
distinct artistic products. The beta should help him complete real weekly work,
not demonstrate abstract AI features.

## Product promise

Tell the app which campaign matters. It should show the next useful action,
find credible opportunities, connect them to the right relationships and
materials, prepare drafts for approval, and keep dates and follow-ups visible.

## Core principles

- Campaign first: every opportunity, draft, task, contact, and date has a purpose.
- Plain language: the user should not need CRM or automation vocabulary.
- Evidence before recommendation: show why an opportunity fits and link its source.
- One dominant action: each major screen should make the next step obvious.
- Human approval: AI recommends and drafts; it does not send or claim outcomes.
- Honest facts: website-sourced information stays unconfirmed until Hamed reviews it.
- Cost control: paid AI is optional, cached, visible, and capped.

## Do not become

- a generic contact database
- an automatic cold-email sender
- a collection of unrelated AI generators
- a system that confuses proposed dates with confirmed bookings
- a multi-user SaaS before the single-user workflow proves useful

## Beta success

The beta succeeds when Hamed uses it consistently for four weeks and can point
to concrete work it helped move forward: opportunities found, outreach drafted,
follow-ups completed, bookings progressed, or campaign decisions clarified.
